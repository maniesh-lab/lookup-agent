# Returns Policy

Lumen Lane wants you to be happy with every order. This page explains when and how you can send something back.

## Return window

You can return most items within 30 days of the delivery date. The item must be unused and in its original packaging, with any tags still attached. Returns that arrive after the 30-day window are not accepted unless the item is covered by the warranty or the damaged-item policy.

## Who pays for return shipping

Return shipping is free when the return is our fault: the item is defective, it arrived damaged, or we sent you the wrong item (wrong product, size or colour). In those cases we email you a prepaid return label.

For any other return, such as a change of mind or an item that simply doesn't suit you, return shipping costs a flat 6.95 USD. We deduct this from your refund instead of charging your card. Premium members get free return labels on every return, including change-of-mind returns.

## Items that cannot be returned

Opened beauty and personal-care products (for example face wash, serums and hand cream), gift cards, and items marked "final sale" cannot be returned unless they are defective.

## How to start a return

Open a support ticket with the topic "refund", include your order number and the reason for the return, and our team will reply with instructions and, if it applies, a prepaid label. Pack the item securely and drop it off with the courier within 7 days of receiving the instructions.

## Exchanges

We don't do direct exchanges. Return the item for a refund and place a new order for the item you want.
