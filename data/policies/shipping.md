# Shipping Policy

This page explains how fast we ship, how much it costs, and what the order statuses mean.

## Processing time

Every order is packed and handed to the courier within 2 business days of being placed. While it is being packed, its status is "processing". Once the courier collects it, the status changes. An order that is still "processing" more than 2 business days after it was placed is delayed: please open a support ticket with the topic "delivery" and we will look into it.

## Standard shipping

Standard shipping usually takes 3 to 5 business days after the order ships. International deliveries outside Nepal can take up to 8 business days. Standard shipping costs 5.99 USD.

## Express shipping

Express delivery is available for an extra fee of 12.99 USD and usually arrives within 1 to 2 business days after the order ships. Express is available in all cities we deliver to.

## Free shipping

Standard shipping is free for every customer on orders of 75 USD or more.

Premium members get free standard shipping on every order over 25 USD, and express shipping for a reduced fee of 4.99 USD. The order total is checked one order at a time; totals from different orders are never combined.

## Order statuses

- processing: we are packing your order
- delivered: the courier confirmed delivery
- cancelled: the order was cancelled and will not ship

## Tracking

Once your order ships, we email a tracking link. The courier's tracking page can take up to 24 hours to show the first update.
