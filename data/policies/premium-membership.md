# Premium Membership

Premium is Lumen Lane's paid membership. Customers without it are on the Basic plan, which is free.

## Price and free trial

Premium costs 8.99 USD per month or 89 USD per year. New members get a 14-day free trial. Each customer can use the free trial once. If you cancel before the trial ends, you are not charged anything.

## Benefits

- Free standard shipping on every order over 25 USD
- Express shipping for 4.99 USD instead of 12.99 USD
- Free return labels, including for change-of-mind returns
- Faster refunds, and the option of instant store credit worth 105% of the refund
- An extra 12 months of warranty on top of the standard warranty
- Priority support: replies to tickets within 12 hours

## If a monthly payment fails

If a monthly payment fails, we email you and try the card again after 3 days and again after 7 days. Your Premium benefits stay active during a 7-day grace period that starts on the day of the failed payment. If the payment still hasn't gone through when the grace period ends, your account moves to the Basic plan and Premium benefits are paused. They come back straight away once a payment succeeds. Benefits you would have had during the pause (for example free shipping on an order placed while paused) are not refunded.

## Cancelling

You can cancel at any time from your account page. Monthly members keep their benefits until the end of the month already paid for. Yearly members can cancel within 14 days of a renewal for a full refund of that renewal.
