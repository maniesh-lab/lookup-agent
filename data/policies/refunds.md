# Refund Policy

This page explains how refunds are paid and how long they take.

## When we issue a refund

We refund an order after we receive the returned item and check its condition. The check usually takes 1 to 2 business days after the parcel reaches our warehouse. Refunds always go back to the original payment method.

## How long refunds take to reach your card

Once we issue the refund, your bank or card provider usually takes 5 to 10 business days to show it on your statement. From the day the return reaches us, most customers see the money within about two weeks. If 10 business days have passed since our refund email and you still don't see it, contact your bank first and then open a ticket with us.

## Cancelled orders

If an order is cancelled before it ships, we refund the full amount automatically within 2 business days. You don't need to open a ticket.

## Shipping fees

Original shipping fees are not refunded, except when the return is our fault (a defective, damaged or wrong item). The flat 6.95 USD return-shipping fee for change-of-mind returns is deducted from the refund.

## Refunds for Premium members

Premium members get faster refunds. We issue a Premium member's refund as soon as the courier scans the returned parcel, without waiting for it to reach the warehouse and be checked. Premium members can also choose store credit instead of a refund; store credit is added instantly and is worth 105% of the refund amount. Change-of-mind returns are free for Premium members, so nothing is deducted for return shipping.

## Partial refunds

If an item comes back used, damaged by the customer, or missing parts, we may refund only part of the price. We will email photos and explain the amount before issuing a partial refund.
