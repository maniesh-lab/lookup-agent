# Warranty Policy

## What the warranty covers

Every electronics and home product we sell comes with a 1-year limited warranty against manufacturing defects, starting on the delivery date. If a covered product stops working because of a defect, we repair it, replace it, or refund it, at our choice. Premium members get an extra 12 months, so their warranty lasts 2 years.

## What the warranty does not cover

The warranty does not cover accidental damage (such as drops or cracked screens), water damage, normal wear and tear, damage from misuse or unauthorised repairs, or cosmetic marks that don't affect how the product works.

## Gifts

The warranty belongs to the product, not to the buyer, so products bought as gifts are covered in the same way. The person who received the gift can make the claim themselves. To make a claim on a gift, show either the order number, the gift receipt, or the email address of the person who bought it so we can find the order.

## What you need to make a claim

- Proof of purchase: the order number, a gift receipt, or the buyer's email address
- A short description of the problem
- Photos or a short video showing the defect
- The serial number, for electronics

## How to make a claim

Open a support ticket with the topic "quality" and include everything listed above. We reply within 48 hours (12 hours for Premium members). If we need the product back, we send a prepaid label; you never pay shipping for a warranty claim.
