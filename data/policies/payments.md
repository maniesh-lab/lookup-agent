# Payments and Prices

## Currency

All prices on Lumen Lane are in US dollars (USD), and every order is charged in USD. If your card is in another currency, your bank converts the amount at its own exchange rate and may add a foreign-transaction fee. We don't control those rates or fees.

## Payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, and eSewa for customers in Nepal. We don't accept cash on delivery.

## When you are charged

Your card is charged when you place the order. If the order is cancelled before it ships, the charge is refunded in full within 2 business days.

## Card security

Payments are handled by our payment provider. Lumen Lane never sees or stores full card numbers.

## Failed payments

If a payment for an order fails, the order is not placed and you can try again with another method. For failed Premium membership payments, see the Premium membership policy.
