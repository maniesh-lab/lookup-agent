# Damaged Item Policy

This policy is for items that arrive broken, cracked, crushed, scratched or otherwise damaged when you open the parcel.

## Reporting window

Report a damaged item within 14 days of the delivery date. Open a support ticket with the topic "damaged_item" and include your order number.

## What to include

Send photos of the damaged item and of the outer box and packaging. Photos of the packaging help us claim the cost back from the courier, so please keep the box until the ticket is closed.

## What we offer

For a damaged item reported within 14 days, you choose between a free replacement and a full refund of that item, including its share of the shipping cost. You never pay return shipping for a damaged item. For damaged items worth less than 20 USD, we usually don't ask you to send them back.

## What this policy does not cover

Damage that happens after delivery, for example from dropping the item or from normal use, is not covered here. If a product develops a fault later, see the warranty policy instead. Damage reported more than 14 days after delivery is handled under the warranty policy, which covers manufacturing defects only.
