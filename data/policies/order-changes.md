# Changing or Cancelling an Order

## Changing the delivery address

You can change the delivery address while the order is still "processing", which is usually up to 2 business days after placing it. Change it on your account page, or open a support ticket with the topic "delivery" and the new address.

Once the order has shipped, we can no longer change the address ourselves. We can ask the courier to redirect the parcel; most couriers accept a redirect if it is requested at least a day before delivery, but we can't promise it.

## If the parcel is already out for delivery

When the parcel is already out for delivery, the address can't be changed. Your options are:

- Use the courier's tracking link to ask them to hold the parcel at a nearby pickup point
- Ask a neighbour or someone at the address to receive it for you
- Refuse the delivery, in which case the parcel comes back to us and we refund the order minus the original shipping fee

## Cancelling an order

You can cancel an order for free while it is still "processing". Cancelled orders are refunded in full within 2 business days. Once an order has shipped it can't be cancelled; instead, return it under the returns policy when it arrives.

## Changing items in an order

We can't add or remove items once an order is placed. Cancel the order while it is still processing and place a new one.
