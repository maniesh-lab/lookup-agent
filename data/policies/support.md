# Customer Support

## How to contact us

Open a support ticket from your account page or by emailing support@lumenlane.example. Every ticket gets one topic: delivery, refund, damaged_item or quality.

## Response times

We reply to tickets within 48 hours. Premium members get priority support, with replies within 12 hours. Our team works every day from 9:00 to 18:00 Nepal time.

## Ticket status

A ticket is "open" while we are working on it and "closed" once the issue is resolved. If a closed ticket's problem comes back, reply to the ticket email within 30 days and it will be reopened.

## What we can't help with by chat

For privacy, we never discuss an order with anyone other than the customer who placed it, and we never ask for a full card number or password.
